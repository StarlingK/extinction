# Aliens vs Predator: Extinction PC Port

> This is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by 20th Century Studios, Disney, Zono, Electronic Arts or Microsoft. *Aliens*, *Predator* and *Aliens versus Predator* are trademarks of their respective owners. You must provide your own dump of the Xbox version.

 - **Runs natively on Windows 10 and 11.**
 - **Direct3D 11 rendering**
 - **Mouse and keyboard controls**
 - **Co-op campaign(Not fully tested)**

 - Controls:
   Z or Left Click  select.
   X or Right Click order.
   E for context ability, holding 1 shows alternate context.
   Enter start.
   Backspace back.
   Q / E White and Black respectively.
   1 / 3 Left, right trigger.
   Arrows keys, D-pad.
   Shift/Ctrl left and right stick click.

> I don't really have much free time to work on this so it will be released as is and probably not updated. The current release is "playable" although it's missing some features like movement markers(due to the renderer pipeline.); bugs like needing to double right click to carry or attack and mostly serves as a open source proof of concept for those who want to work on a recomp of this game.
